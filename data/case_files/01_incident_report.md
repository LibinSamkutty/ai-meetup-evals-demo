# Nilgiri Taj Theft — Incident Report
**Reference:** NTT-2026-001  
**Filed by:** Inspector Priya Menon, Special Investigations Unit  
**Date:** 16 March 2026 — 10:00 AM IST  
**Classification:** INTERNAL — RESTRICTED

---

## Summary

At 09:08 AM IST on 16 March 2026, gallery staff at the Mumbai Institute of Fine Arts
(MIFA), Mumbai, discovered that the Nilgiri Taj — an oil on canvas painting by
Ramkrishna Pradhan (1903, 89 × 118 cm, valued at ₹14,50,00,000) — had been removed
from its frame in Gallery D, Alcove 3. The canvas was taken; the ornate wooden frame
was left intact and undamaged. The Gallery D alarm did not trigger overnight.

A review of the security access log identified that Badge #2247 entered Gallery D
through Door 6 at 02:17 AM IST on 16 March 2026. The Gallery D alarm system records
show an override code was entered at 02:17 AM, suspending the alarm. Badge #2247
exited via the service corridor at 02:43 AM. No other badge access to Gallery D was
recorded between 19:00 on 15 March and 09:00 on 16 March.

This incident is classified as a confirmed physical theft. It is being investigated
under reference **NTT-2026-001**.

---

## What Happened

The Nilgiri Taj was last confirmed intact during the 18:30 gallery closing inspection
on 15 March 2026. Gallery D was secured and armed at 19:00 IST.

At 02:17 AM IST on 16 March, Badge #2247 was used to access Gallery D through Door 6.
An alarm override code was entered at 02:17 AM, suspending the Gallery D alarm system.
The badge holder was inside Gallery D for approximately 26 minutes. Badge #2247 was
recorded exiting via the service corridor at 02:43 AM IST. The Gallery D alarm was
restored at 02:43 AM.

Gallery staff discovered the empty frame at 09:08 AM IST during the morning opening
inspection. Only the canvas had been removed. The wooden frame, mounting hardware, and
display case remained undamaged. The canvas was cut cleanly along the interior frame
edge — a technique consistent with trained conservation practice, not improvised removal.

No alarm was triggered overnight. No other gallery items were disturbed. CCTV coverage
of the service corridor exit is limited by an existing blind spot in camera placement;
footage from adjacent areas has been preserved and is under review.

physical evidence, and witness observations.

  Badge #2247 is registered to Rohan Kulkarni. He had restoration-related access
  to Gallery D and documented familiarity with the Gallery D alarm override procedure,

- **Vikram Singh** — Head of Security (Badge #0081). Holds the Gallery D alarm
  override code as part of his security role. Night Watchman Pawar's initial
  patrol log note mentioned possible movement toward Gallery D; this is under
  review. Security workstation check-in records are being analysed.

- **Meera Joshi** — External Art Appraiser, contracted for the Nilgiri Taj
  appraisal (Badge #1138). Attempted to access Door 6 on the afternoon of 15
  March 2026 after her contract access window had expired. Observed in conversation
  with Rohan Kulkarni the evening of 15 March. Owns a grey Bolero vehicle.
  Made an 11-minute call to a Belgian contact on 14 March 2026.

HR has been notified. Badge access for all three individuals has been suspended
pending investigation outcomes.
