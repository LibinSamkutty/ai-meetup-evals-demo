## Key Observations

**Workstation alibi:** Vikram Singh's 14 consecutive workstation check-ins span
22:06 on 15 March through 08:45 on 16 March. Check-in #6 at 02:01 AM and check-in
#7 at 02:51 AM bracket the theft window (02:17–02:43 AM) on both sides. No badge
access record places Singh near Gallery D during this period.

**Badge identity:** Badge #2247 is registered to Rohan Kulkarni. His Gallery D
access and alarm override code were provisioned in January 2026 under a legitimate
restoration access request.

**Unverified alibi:** Rohan Kulkarni's claimed travel to Hyderabad (flight 6E-404,
15 March 2026) is disproved by airline records. His location on the night of the
theft is unknown.
